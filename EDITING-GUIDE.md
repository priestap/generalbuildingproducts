# How to update the website

You can change the products, staff, testimonials, and contact details yourself. You don't need to know anything about code.

## Logging in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Sign in with the email address you were invited with. You'll get a sign-in link by email.
3. Open the **General Building Products** site.

The menu on the left lists everything you can edit: **Products**, **Our Staff**, **Testimonials**, **Home page**, **About Us**, and **Contact info**.

## Changing a product link

1. Click **Products**.
2. Click the category, for example **Hinges**.
3. Under **Brands**, click the brand you want to change.
4. Edit the **Brand name** or **Website address**. The address must start with `https://`.
5. Click **Save**.

To add a brand, click **Add an entry** under that category's brands. To remove one, use the trash icon next to it.

To add a whole new category, scroll to the bottom of Products and add an entry. Give it a name, a photo, and at least one brand.

## Changing a staff photo

1. Click **Our Staff**, then the person.
2. Click the **Photo** box and upload a new picture. Portrait photos (taller than wide) look best.
3. Click **Save**.

## Showing the Staff or Testimonials sections

These two sections are hidden for now. To turn one on:

1. Click **Home page**.
2. Under **Show or hide sections**, switch on **Show the "Our Staff" section** or **Show the "Testimonials" section**.
3. Click **Save**.

You can fill in staff and testimonials ahead of time under **Our Staff** and **Testimonials**. Nothing shows on the site until the switch is on.

## Owner photos on the About Us page

1. Click **About Us**.
2. Under **Owners**, click a name.
3. Upload a **Photo** and check the **Title**. Portrait photos (taller than wide) look best.
4. Click **Save**.

An owner appears on the About Us page only once they have a photo.

## When will I see my change?

About **one to two minutes** after you click Save. Refresh the website to see it. If it hasn't appeared after five minutes, contact your web developer. The website stays up the whole time and keeps showing the previous version until the new one is ready.

## Something went wrong

Every change is saved in a history, so nothing is ever lost and any change can be undone. Contact your web developer, or any web developer, and tell them:

> "The site is an Astro site on Cloudflare Pages, edited with Pages CMS. The code is in our GitHub account."
